What to look at

  • Layouts switch on the slide's own shape: C portrait (under 0.85), B square-ish (0.85 to 1.15), A landscape (1.15 and up, card beside the product; wide version from 1.6). C compact puts ENQUIRE NOW beside the price on phones and on the squarer portrait boxes.
  • The red cashback starburst sits at the lower right of the product on both slides, as on the current General artwork.
  • Use the date menu to test expiry: slide 2 drops after 11 Dec 2026, and with both gone the left panel takes the full row.
  • The sunburst rays turn slowly (one turn every two minutes) and stop when reduced motion is set.
  • The General product image is the one Gemin3 supplied. The MHI image is cut out of the current live MHI slide.
